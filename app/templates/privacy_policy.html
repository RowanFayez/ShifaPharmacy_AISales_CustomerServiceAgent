Shifa Pharmacy Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Shifa Pharmacy uses this Messenger assistant to answer pharmacy questions, show live product availability and prices, provide delivery information, and help prepare customer orders.

Information we process

  • Messenger Page-scoped ID (PSID) to keep a conversation connected to the same customer.
  • Messages sent to the Page and the replies generated by the assistant.
  • Order details voluntarily provided by a customer, such as recipient name, phone number, and delivery address.

How we use information

We use this information only to operate customer support, answer requests, prepare orders, update inventory, and connect a customer with a pharmacist when needed. We do not sell customer information or use it for unrelated advertising.

Storage and retention

Conversation and order data are stored in the pharmacy application database and are accessible only to authorized pharmacy staff. Data is retained only as needed for customer support, order fulfilment, safety, accounting, and legal obligations.

Medical safety

The assistant does not diagnose conditions or provide personalized dosage instructions. Medical questions can be escalated to a pharmacist.

Your choices

You may request access to or deletion of your Messenger conversation data by sending a message to the Shifa Pharmacy Page with the subject “Data deletion request”. See our data deletion instructions.

Contact

Contact Shifa Pharmacy through the official Facebook Page for privacy questions or deletion requests.
